# Archive — CEO / founders quiz

The original vertical. **No source, copy, or analytics for it exists in this repo** (it was
a single commit with an 11-byte README), so there is nothing here to learn from yet.

## Why it was replaced

Scored against the factory gates in `playbook/QUIZ-FACTORY.md`, founders fail the gates
that matter most:

| Gate | Founders | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dated pain | **Weak** | No scheduled, high-stakes event that a diagnostic attaches to |
| Status at stake | Strong | Founder identity is highly shareable |
| Credible rubric | **Weak** | No industry-agreed rubric for "good CEO"; any axes read as invented |
| Proven spend | **Weak** | Diffuse — coaching exists but there's no standard price to anchor to |
| Public watering hole | Strong | Founder Twitter/LinkedIn, accelerator communities |

2/5 strong. That's the finding worth carrying forward, and it's precisely why
system design interviews are the better bet: a scheduled event, a rubric the industry
already agrees on, and documented spend of $47–$279 on courses plus $90–120 per human mock.
